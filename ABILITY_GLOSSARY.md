### What the cards say

- **Bleed** (`kit_bleed`): Serrated strikes open a wound: 40% of Attack as direct damage over 6 seconds, ignoring Armor.
- **Overcharge** (`kit_shock`): Every hit arcs high voltage: 45% of Attack as energy damage over 5 seconds.
- **Thermal Pulse** (`kit_burn`): Incendiary payload ignites the target: 35% of Attack as burn damage over 6 seconds.
